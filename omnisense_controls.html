Hand Gestures (requires Camera ON)
✋
OPEN PALM — Superman Pose
Hold your hand flat, all five fingers extended toward the camera. Hover near a shape to automatically grab it, then move your hand to move the object in 3D space.
GRAB & MOVE
✊
FIST — Close Hand
Close all fingers into a fist. If currently holding an object, the object is dropped at its current position. If an object is selected but not held, it is deleted from the scene.
DROP / DELETE
🤏
PINCH — Thumb + Index
Bring your thumb tip and index finger tip close together (pinch). The nearest 3D object within reach is selected and highlighted in the properties panel.
SELECT NEAREST
👆
POINT — Index Finger Only
Extend only your index finger while keeping the other fingers curled. Points at and selects the nearest object in the direction of your finger.
SELECT NEAREST
✌️
PEACE — Index & Middle Out
Extend your index and middle fingers in a V/peace sign. A random 3D primitive (sphere, box, cone, torus, or cylinder) is instantly spawned at the center of your palm position in the scene.
SPAWN SHAPE
🤲
TWO HANDS — Open Both Palms
Both hands open simultaneously near the same object. The shape moves toward the midpoint between your two palms. Moving hands apart / together controls position fluidly with two-hand tracking.
TWO-HAND MOVE
💡 Tip: The gesture engine tracks both hands independently at up to 60 FPS using MediaPipe on GPU. Ensure good lighting facing your hand for best detection accuracy. A green skeleton overlay confirms tracking is active.
Voice Commands — Groq AI Intelligence (Mic must be ON)

Speech is transcribed live into the input field. Commands are sent to Groq LLaMA with full scene context — the AI understands object names, positions, and your environment.

🎙️
"spawn a sphere to the left of the cube"
→ AI calculates position relative to Cube.001 and spawns a sphere there
🎙️
"delete the torus"
→ AI identifies Torus.001 and removes it from the scene
🎙️
"clear the scene" or "reset everything"
→ All objects removed from viewport
🎙️
"switch to infinite space"
→ Space mode toggles to Infinite Space with orbit controls
🎙️
"what objects are in the scene right now?"
→ AI lists all objects, their types and current coordinates
🎙️
"spawn a cone on top of the sphere"
→ AI reads Sphere.001's Y position and places cone above it
⚠️ Note: Voice transcription appears live in the text input box as you speak. You can edit the text before pressing SEND, or just say your command fully and the system will auto-process it.

Viewport Quick Controls (top-right of 3D viewport)
📹
CAM ON / OFF
Toggles the live webcam feed overlaid behind the 3D scene. Also activates real-time hand tracking via MediaPipe.
Hardware
🌍
SCAN ENV
Captures the current camera frame and sends it to Groq Vision AI to analyse your physical room — detects surfaces, objects and depth for spatial calibration.
Groq Vision
➕
BOX / SPH / CON / TOR / CYL
One-click buttons to instantly spawn each primitive type (Box, Sphere, Cone, Torus, Cylinder) at a random position in the current scene.

Space Modes (top bar toggle)
🏠
Room Space
The 3D grid is positioned at estimated floor level. The camera FOV is locked to 60° matching your webcam, so virtual objects appear to sit in your physical room. No free orbit — the scene stays anchored to your environment.
AR Mode
🌌
Infinite Space
Orbit controls enabled — click & drag to rotate the 3D viewport freely. The infinite grid extends in all directions. Use this for pure 3D modelling without the AR constraint.
